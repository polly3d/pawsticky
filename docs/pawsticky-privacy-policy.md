**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

PawSticky ("the app") is published by FitPetGo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to the PawSticky iOS app, its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, its Live Activities, and its Apple Watch app. It does not apply to the FitPetGo website, which has its own privacy policy.

## 1. Summary

**PawSticky does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking. Everything you create stays on your devices.

## 2. What the app stores, and where

The app stores your notes, checklists, reminders, pet choice, and growth progress. This data lives **only on your device**, in storage shared between the app, its widgets, and its Apple Watch extension so that they can show the same information. The data is protected by iOS data protection (it is unavailable until you unlock your device for the first time after a restart).

We have no server that receives this data, so **we cannot see, recover, or delete it**.

## 3. What we collect

**Nothing.** The app contains no network code that sends your notes or any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. It uses no analytics or advertising services and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 4. Permissions the app may ask for

- **Notifications (optional).** If you set a reminder on a note, iOS asks whether the app may send notifications. Reminders are scheduled **locally on your device**; nothing is sent to a server.
- The app does **not** request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health data.

## 5. Features that involve other services you choose

- **Apple Watch.** Your notes and pet are synced between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's system connection between your own devices. No cloud service of ours is involved.
- **Sharing.** When you share a note card, the app creates an image and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The app or service you pick (for example Messages, or "Save Image" to your photo library) receives that image and handles it under its own privacy policy.
- **Backups.** When you choose to back up your notes, the app creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and you decide where to save or send it. We cannot recover your passphrase, and we never receive your backup file.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Notes you delete or edit may remain in an on-device history for up to **7 days** so that you can undo a mistake; after that they are permanently removed.
- To delete all of your data, delete the app from your device (and, if you used Apple Watch, remove it from the Watch). Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you created and saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

## 7. Third-party code

The app includes the open-source SQLite wrapper GRDB (MIT license) to store data locally on your device. It does not collect or transmit data.

## 8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
